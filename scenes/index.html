YOUR FIELD MANUAL

Observe. Decide. Act.

Select a mission, confirm its release, and tap numbered objects to inspect them. Choose a tool from your kit, then click a scene object to use it. You can also use the inspection panel’s next-action button.

  1. Confirm release and authority.
  2. Inspect and photograph before acting.
  3. Check the scenario-approved kit and work boundary.
  4. Match your response to the material and finding.
  5. Document every object, including referrals, then submit the report.

All controls work with touch or keyboard Tab and Enter. Your progress is saved on this device. There is no countdown; careful judgment matters more than speed.

A simulation, not a certification. Fictional scenarios simplify complex work. Do not enter real hazardous scenes based on this game. Real PPE, respiratory protection, chemical use, disposal and clearance require qualified assessment, training, applicable rules and product instructions. Unknown substances, sharps, electrical or structural risks call for stopping and qualified review.
